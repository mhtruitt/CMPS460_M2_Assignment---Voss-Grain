How a piece gets made

Three stages, usually eight to twelve weeks from first sketch to delivery.

  1. 1

    Sketch and spec

    We talk through how the piece will actually be used, then I draw it to scale and price the wood before anything is cut.

  2. 2

    Select the boards

    I pick each board by hand from the rack, matching grain across a top or a set of doors before a single joint is cut.

  3. 3

    Shape, join, finish

    Joinery is cut by hand where it shows, glued up flat, then finished in three coats of hardwax oil.

About Maya

I trained as a cabinetmaker's apprentice outside Asheville and opened my own one-person shop in 2012. Most weeks I'm splitting time between the bench and the sawmill down the road, picking through logs before they're ever milled into lumber.

I build almost everything to commission, in small batches, using species I can trace back to a specific forest. If a piece breaks, I want it to be worth fixing — so I build it that way from the start.
